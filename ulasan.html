GALERI WARISAN MAYA

"Walking through time, stepping into culture."

BUTIRAN PROJEK / PROJECT DETAIL

Sebuah pengalaman galeri realiti maya (VR) di mana pengunjung berjalan terus melalui dewan pameran maya. Pameran interaktif di sekeliling ruang menghidupkan tiga tunjang warisan persembahan Malaysia melalui audio dan visual yang teraktif mengikut jarak.

An immersive VR exhibition hall where visitors physically walk through a virtual gallery. Interactive showcases bring three pillars of Malaysian performance heritage to life through proximity-triggered spatial audio and visuals — turning passive video-viewing into an active, intimate cultural encounter.

TARIANTraditional Dance
NYANYIANTraditional Singing
